# Demo Script — FAULTLINE XR (3 minutes)

## Before recording

- Deploy `web/dist/` to the public HTTPS URL.
- Test the full CASCADE flow in Quest Browser.
- Keep the headset and browser ready.
- Record in immersive mode and lead with the spatial workspace.

## 0:00–0:25 — Hook and entry

1. Show the FAULTLINE XR welcome screen.
2. Tap **How pinch works** briefly to establish the hands-first premise.
3. Open **CASCADE** and begin the scenario.

## 0:25–0:55 — Incident and investigation

1. Show the spatial service graph with Database down and App Service failing.
2. Open the incident.
3. Dispatch **TRACE** and **ORACLE**.
4. Show evidence, confidence values, and recommendations.

## 0:55–1:40 — Decision and remediation

1. Synthesise the findings.
2. Propose `RestartService`.
3. Emphasise that execution requires an explicit approval.
4. Approve and execute the remediation.

## 1:40–2:25 — Verification and resolution

1. Show health recovering across the dependency graph.
2. Tap **Verify recovery**.
3. Resolve the incident.
4. Show the after-action report.

## 2:25–3:00 — Differentiators

1. Show two-hand graph scaling.
2. Briefly show accessibility settings.
3. End on the resolved spatial workspace.

## Key narration points

- Hands are the primary input; controllers are never required.
- Agents are deterministic and local; no API key or external service is needed.
- Remediation is guarded by explicit approval, checkpointing, and verification.
- The experience is seated, stationary, and designed for a short complete run.

Upload the recording to YouTube or Vimeo as a **public** video under three minutes. Add the link to the Devpost submission form and `submission/BUILD_MANIFEST.json`.
